# Third-party resources and licenses

**Play Along** is developed as a non-commercial application. This does not remove attribution, ShareAlike, or individual-score licensing requirements.

The repository includes **no redistributed third-party musical scores**. Online catalog entries are links to their respective providers.

- [ASAP Dataset](https://github.com/fosfrancesco/asap-dataset) — CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; attribute the dataset authors and retain license terms. This version links to score/MIDI files, not bundled binary copies.
- [MuseTrainer Library](https://github.com/musetrainer/library) — individual scores and arrangements may have differing rights; verify before redistribution.
- [Mutopia Project](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/) — per-item license may vary. Inspect individual works.
- [OpenSheetMusicDisplay (OSMD)](https://github.com/opensheetmusicdisplay/opensheetmusicdisplay) — third-party library loaded on demand through CDN; inspect repository/package license before redistributing a vendored copy. No OSMD JavaScript is bundled in this project.

The current source app originated from Play Along v0.9.1; code attribution/ownership remains with its respective author(s). This migration is a structural reorganization, not a new license grant.
